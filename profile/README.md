# Synos Labs

**Applied AI, built for East Africa.**

We're an applied AI research lab in Nairobi, researching how AI works for the markets, languages and phones people in East Africa actually use. Every experiment gets written up as it runs.

## How we work

- **We experiment in public.** Every test gets a lab note: what worked, what didn't, and what we changed.
- **Small experiments, written up.** One narrow question at a time, on real local data, published before we move on.
- **Built for here.** Local languages, local markets, mobile data and WhatsApp, designed for how East Africa works, not as an afterthought.

## Current research

📈 **NSE annual report processing** *(in progress)*
Testing how well current AI models extract figures, dividends and risks from the annual reports of companies listed on the Nairobi Securities Exchange, with every answer checked by hand.
